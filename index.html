HY-KERS HYBRID SYSTEM

V12 meets Formula 1 electrification.

LaFerrari was the first Ferrari road car built around hybrid technology, pairing a naturally aspirated 6.3-litre V12 with an electric motor drawn from the brand's Formula 1 Kinetic Energy Recovery System.

The electric motor delivers 161 hp and 199 lb-ft at any engine speed, filling in torque exactly where a naturally aspirated V12 would otherwise fall off, while regenerative braking keeps the battery topped up without adding a plug.

Combined, the two power sources produce 950 hp and 664 lb-ft of torque, sent to the rear wheels alone through a 7-speed dual-clutch gearbox shared on one side with the primary motor-generator.

LINEAGE

The successor to a short, famous list.

LaFerrari joined the line of Ferrari halo cars built once a decade to show what the company can do without limits.

1987 — F40

Ferrari's first true hypercar, built to mark the company's 40th anniversary.

1995 — F50

A road car built around an F1-derived V12, closer to a race car than any Ferrari before it.

2002 — Enzo

Named for the company's founder, and the first Ferrari road car with an F1-style semi-automatic gearbox as standard.

2013 — LaFerrari

The first hybrid in the lineage, and Ferrari's flagship for the modern era.
